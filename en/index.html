THE CORE TRIAD

Three distinct responsibilities

Natural Trading Theory is not a search for one universal price level. It separates evidence by responsibility: location cannot replace direction, and a time window cannot replace energy analysis.

ENERGY / DIRECTION01

First, identify who is moving the market

Read energy as a continuous sequence of expansion, decay, rest, and takeover. Energy establishes direction, but one side fading does not prove that the other side has taken control.

FIB / SPACE02

Then, locate price in its spatial context

Fib retracement or channels define price space and areas of attraction. Retracement locates the current swing; channels add a longer-cycle spatial view. Neither is an automatic reversal trigger.

TREND / TIME03

Finally, wait for a volatility window

Fib trend time marks windows in which volatility may expand. It tells us when to pay closer attention, but it does not predict direction on its own.

THE DECISION CHAIN

Not stacked signals.
A handoff of responsibilities.

If one step has no clear conclusion, the next step cannot manufacture the missing answer. Price space cannot create direction, and a candle cannot erase the energy backdrop.

  1. M / H / L01

    Energy establishes one directional bias

    The main reference is closed 4H volume, read as continuous bullish and bearish energy waves. M describes the longer-cycle primary-energy backdrop; H/L describe shorter-cycle expansion, rest, exhaustion, and opposing accumulation.

  2. RETRACE / CHANNEL02

    Fib retracement or channels test the price space

    Retracement locates the current swing and its attraction areas. Channels support longer-cycle spatial observation and require confirmation across multiple swings. A touch, a channel line, or a channel failure cannot establish a reversal by itself.

  3. 0.618 · 1 · 1.61803

    Trend time sets attention windows

    The rhythm is calibrated with historical candles that carried meaningful volume and movement. A time line answers “when is closer attention warranted?”—it does not answer “which way will price move?”

  4. 4H → 2H / 1H04

    Closed candles confirm action

    Ordinary, relatively strong, absolutely strong, and balanced candles refine very short-term execution. Lower-timeframe confirmation cannot overturn the higher-timeframe energy backdrop, and an open candle is never treated as final evidence.

  5. RISK FIRST05

    Define the cost of being wrong first

    Analysis may contain uncertainty; execution cannot be arbitrary. Before entering, define what invalidates the idea, the maximum acceptable loss, and whether the decision is being driven by chasing or by the urge to win back a loss.

FOUR BOUNDARIES

Four boundaries that must stay clear

A public definition must state what the theory does not do. Stable boundaries keep hindsight from explaining every possible outcome as if it had always fit the theory.

BOUNDARY 01

Time is a reminder, not a directional signal

Trend time marks candidate volatility windows. It is not a direction tool, and reaching a time line does not guarantee a reversal.

BOUNDARY 02

Decay is not reversal

A fading move only shows that the existing advantage is weakening. Reversal requires the opposing side to accumulate and complete a takeover.

BOUNDARY 03

M is context, not a trigger

Primary energy describes the market state; it is not an instant entry button. Space, confirmation, and risk conditions still have to align.

BOUNDARY 04

When evidence is incomplete, wait

If energy conflicts, the Fib retracement or channel is distorted, time evidence is absent, or price sits in the middle, waiting is itself a valid conclusion.

HOW WE RECORD

A record before the outcome

Each public case keeps the original view, publication time, conditions for validation, and conditions for invalidation. After the move, the review cites the original record whether the analysis worked or failed.

A public case archive will open after enough genuine pre-event records exist. Historical charts remain study reviews and are not mixed into the pre-event evidence set.
